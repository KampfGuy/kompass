Kugel Mail help

Getting started

Pick Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo or Other, type your email and password, and press Test & save. Kugel Mail checks both receiving (IMAP) and sending (SMTP) before saving.

Gmail: use an App Password

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification in your Google Account.
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Create one named "Kugel Mail" and paste the 16 letters (spaces don't matter) as the password.

Your normal Gmail password will not work — Google blocks it for mail apps.

Yahoo

Account Security → Generate app password, then paste it.

Outlook / Hotmail

Microsoft now requires "modern" (OAuth) sign-in for personal Outlook.com accounts. Kugel Mail 1.0 only supports passwords, so Outlook.com may refuse to connect. Some work/school accounts still allow passwords.

Privacy & safety

  • Your password is encrypted with Windows' own protection (DPAPI) and stays on this PC. If that is not available, it is only kept until you close the app.
  • Messages are shown in a locked box: no scripts, no forms, and pictures from the internet are blocked until you press Load images.
  • Links open in your normal browser.
  • Kugel Mail talks only to your mail servers — nothing else.
